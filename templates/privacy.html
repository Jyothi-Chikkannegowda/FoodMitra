🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026 · Your privacy is important to us

1. 📋 Information We Collect
When you register on FoodMitra, we collect:
  • Personal information — Name, email, phone number
  • Location information — Address and GPS coordinates for 5km matching
  • For cooks — Aadhaar number for identity verification
  • Order history and payment transaction IDs
  • Profile photos uploaded by users
2. 🔐 How We Use Your Information
We use your information to:
  • Match customers with home cooks within 5km radius
  • Process orders and payments securely
  • Send order confirmation and bill emails
  • Verify home cook identity through Aadhaar
  • Improve our platform and user experience
3. 🛡️ Data Security
We take data security seriously:
  • All passwords are encrypted using PBKDF2 SHA256 hashing
  • Aadhaar numbers are masked — only last 4 digits shown
  • Payments are handled by Razorpay — we never store card details
  • All data is stored securely in our MySQL database
4. 📍 Location Data
FoodMitra uses your address to calculate distance between cooks and customers. This is used only for the 5km radius feature to show nearby food. Your exact coordinates are never shared publicly — only the cook's general area is shown to customers.
5. 🤝 Data Sharing
We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We only share:
  • Delivery address with the cook for home delivery orders
  • Cook's pickup address with customer for self pickup orders
  • Transaction data with Razorpay for payment processing
6. 🍪 Cookies
FoodMitra uses session cookies to keep you logged in. These cookies are essential for the platform to function. No third party tracking cookies are used.
7. ✏️ Your Rights
You have the right to:
  • Update your profile information at any time
  • Delete your account by contacting support
  • Request a copy of your personal data
  • Opt out of email notifications
8. 📞 Contact Us
For any privacy concerns or data requests, contact us at foodmitra.support@gmail.com
